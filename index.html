04. Engineering Projects

★ FEATURED · FINAL YEAR PROJECT

TEG & PCM-Based Hybrid Heat Exchanger for Solar Panels

This final-year project focuses on the design and development of a hybrid solar thermal system integrating Thermoelectric Generators (TEG) and Phase Change Materials (PCM) with a solar plate / heat-exchanger configuration. It explores improved utilization of solar thermal energy by combining heat-transfer enhancement, thermal energy storage and thermoelectric energy conversion within one integrated system.

TEGs convert a temperature difference directly into electrical energy, while PCMs absorb and store thermal energy during phase transition. Integrating them with a solar thermal plate and heat exchanger, the project investigates improved thermal management, temperature regulation, energy recovery and overall utilization of available solar energy.

🔥Heat-Transfer EnhancementSolar plate + heat-exchanger configuration with heat sinks and cooling fans
🧊Thermal Energy StorageParaffin PCM absorbs and stores heat during phase transition
